AirFiber Refund Policy
Effective date: 1 August 2026

Customers may request a refund within 7 days of first installation if measured speed stays below 50% of the subscribed plan during three official speed tests.

Refunds are not available for:
- Partial months on prepaid packs after the 7-day window
- Charges for optional OTT add-ons once the promo code is redeemed
- Equipment that is damaged or not returned

Approved refunds are paid back to the original payment method within 10 business days.

To raise a refund request, open a ticket in the AirFiber app and attach the three speed-test screenshots. Support ticket category: Billing > Refund.
