# Account and plan management

## Changing your plan mid-cycle

Prepaid customers can upgrade at any time. The unused value of the current pack is calculated on a pro-rata daily basis and applied as credit against the new pack. Upgrades take effect within 30 minutes.

Downgrades cannot be applied mid-cycle. A downgrade is scheduled for the start of the next billing cycle, and no pro-rata credit is issued for the difference.

## Transferring the connection to a new address

Raise a relocation request at least 7 days before the move. Relocation within the same city is free on all plans. Relocation to a different city is treated as a new installation and is charged at ₹500, and the existing pack is paused for up to 30 days during the move.

## Changing the account holder

Ownership transfer requires a signed transfer form from both parties, photo ID for the incoming account holder, and a nil outstanding balance. Processing takes 3 working days.

## Suspending service temporarily

Prepaid accounts can be paused once per pack for up to 30 days. The remaining validity resumes when the account is reactivated. Postpaid accounts cannot be paused; the monthly rental continues to apply.

## Closing the account

Submit a closure request in the app. Equipment (ONT and mesh extender) must be returned within 14 days of closure, or a non-return charge of ₹2500 applies to the ONT and ₹1500 to the mesh extender.
