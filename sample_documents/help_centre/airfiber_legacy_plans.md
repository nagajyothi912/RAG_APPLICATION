# Legacy and regional AirFiber plans

These packs are closed to new customers but remain active for subscribers who were on them before 1 July 2026. Regional variants are still sold in the cities listed against each pack. For the current retail line-up see the main AirFiber broadband plans article.

## AirFiber_1099_1M plan

The AirFiber_1099_1M plan runs at 150 Mbps with a 1.5 TB monthly data allowance. No mesh extender and no OTT bundle are included. Closed to new customers since 1 April 2026; existing subscribers may continue to renew it monthly.

## AirFiber_1149_1M plan

The AirFiber_1149_1M plan runs at 180 Mbps and allows 2 TB of data per month. A mesh extender is included; an OTT bundle is not. Closed to new customers since 1 June 2026.

## AirFiber_1199_3M plan

The AirFiber_1199_3M plan is a three-month pack billed once at the start of the term. It runs at 150 Mbps with a 2 TB monthly data allowance and includes neither a mesh extender nor an OTT bundle. Customers frequently mistake this pack for the one-month retail pack of a similar price; the two differ in speed, in data allowance, and in what is bundled.

## AirFiber_1249_1M plan

The AirFiber_1249_1M plan runs at 220 Mbps with no monthly data cap. A mesh extender and a 1-month OTT bundle are included. Sold only in Pune and Nagpur.

## AirFiber_1299_1M plan

The AirFiber_1299_1M plan runs at 250 Mbps with no monthly data cap. A mesh extender and a 3-month OTT bundle are included. Sold only in Hyderabad.

## AirFiber_999_1M plan

The AirFiber_999_1M plan runs at 120 Mbps and allows 1 TB of data per month. No mesh extender and no OTT bundle are included. Closed to new customers since 1 January 2026.

## Migrating off a legacy plan

Any legacy pack can be migrated to a current retail plan at the start of the next billing cycle. Pro-rata credit is issued for the unused portion of the legacy pack. Migration is irreversible: a closed pack cannot be re-subscribed once the account has moved off it.
