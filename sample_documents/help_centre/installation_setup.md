# Installation and first-time setup

## Before the appointment

Have the following ready before the engineer arrives:
- A government photo ID matching the account holder name
- A power socket within 2 metres of the intended ONT location
- Permission from the building manager if the cable must cross common areas
- The 6-digit installation OTP sent to the registered mobile number

The engineer cannot complete the installation without the OTP. If the OTP has expired, request a new one from Account > Installation.

## Installation timelines

Free installation within 48 hours applies to the AirFiber_1199_1M and AirFiber_1999_3M plans. The AirFiber_599_1M plan is installed within 5 working days and carries a one-time installation charge of ₹500.

## First-time setup

1. Connect the ONT to power and wait for the status LED to turn solid green.
2. Open the AirFiber app and sign in with the registered mobile number.
3. Select Set up new connection and scan the QR code on the underside of the ONT.
4. Choose a Wi-Fi network name and password. The default password printed on the ONT label must be changed during setup.
5. Register each device MAC address under Account > Devices if MAC whitelisting is enabled on the account.

## Mesh extender placement

Place the mesh extender roughly halfway between the ONT and the weakest-signal room, on a shelf at least 1 metre above the floor, away from microwaves and cordless phone bases. The extender pairs automatically when powered on within 10 metres of the ONT.
