# Billing and payments FAQ

How do I pay for AirFiber?
You can pay by UPI, debit card, credit card, or net banking in the AirFiber app or on airfiber.example.com/pay.

When is my bill generated?
Prepaid plans are charged at the start of the pack. Postpaid bills are generated on the 1st of each month and are due within 7 days.

What happens if payment fails?
If auto-pay fails, we retry twice over 48 hours. After that the connection is moved to a 5-day grace period at reduced speed (2 Mbps). After grace, the line is suspended.

Can I get an invoice?
Yes. PDF invoices are emailed to the registered address and can be downloaded from Account > Invoices.

Is there a late fee?
Postpaid accounts incur a late fee of ₹100 if the bill is unpaid 7 days after the due date. Prepaid packs are not charged a late fee because service simply expires.
