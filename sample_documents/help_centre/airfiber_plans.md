# AirFiber broadband plans

This help-centre article describes current AirFiber prepaid fiber plans.

## AirFiber_1199_1M plan

The AirFiber_1199_1M plan is a one-month prepaid fiber broadband plan priced at ₹1199.

Benefits of the AirFiber_1199_1M plan:
- 200 Mbps unlimited download and upload speed
- Unlimited data with no fair-usage cap
- Complimentary AirFiber mesh extender
- Free installation within 48 hours of payment
- 24/7 priority support on chat, phone, and email
- OTT bundle: Disney+ Hotstar, SonyLIV, and Amazon Prime Video for 1 month
- Static IP available on request at no extra charge
- No traffic shaping between 12:00 AM and 6:00 AM

The plan auto-renews only if the customer enables auto-pay in the AirFiber app. Cancellation is allowed up to 24 hours before the next billing date.

## AirFiber_599_1M plan

The AirFiber_599_1M plan is a one-month prepaid plan priced at ₹599. It includes 100 Mbps speed, 1 TB monthly data, standard installation within 5 working days, and email support. It does not include a mesh extender or OTT bundle.

## AirFiber_1999_3M plan

The AirFiber_1999_3M plan is a three-month prepaid plan priced at ₹1999. It includes 300 Mbps unlimited speed, a mesh extender, and the same OTT bundle as AirFiber_1199_1M for the full 3 months.
